# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project collaboration must remain respectful, professional, and focused on the
work. Participants are expected to give actionable feedback, distinguish facts
from assumptions, protect private information, and treat people fairly across
experience, identity, background, and ability.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, deliberate exposure of
secrets or personal data, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and project-related communication in other spaces controlled by the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the private
maintainer channel available to collaborators. Do not include personal or
sensitive information in a public issue. If no private project channel is
available, use GitHub's content-reporting controls.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled with the minimum
disclosure reasonably necessary to investigate and respond.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not this process.
